- Business users: Rp 999,000/month
- Enterprise users: Rp 3,000,000+/month
- **Blended ARPU**: Rp 500,000/month

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):**
- Organic (content, SEO): Rp 200,000
- Paid (ads): Rp 800,000
- Referral: Rp 100,000

**Lifetime Value (LTV):**
- Average customer lifetime: 24 months
- Average monthly revenue: Rp 500,000
- Gross margin: 70%
- **LTV**: Rp 500,000 × 24 × 0.70 = Rp 8,400,000

**LTV:CAC Ratio**: 8,400,000 / 400,000 = 21:1 ✅ (Excellent!)

**Payback Period**: 400,000 / (500,000 × 0.70) = 1.1 months ✅
