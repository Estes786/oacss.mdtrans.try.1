## 📄 2. LEGAL DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

### A. Individual (Indonesian Resident)

**Dokumen yang Dibutuhkan:**
- ✅ **KTP (Kartu Tanda Penduduk)** - National ID card
- ✅ **NPWP (Nomor Pokok Wajib Pajak)** - Tax Identification Number

**Format:**
- High-quality scan/photo
- Jelas dan terbaca
- Valid (tidak expired)

---

### B. Individual (Foreign / With Residency Permit)

**Dokumen yang Dibutuhkan:**
- ✅ **Passport** - Valid passport
- ✅ **KITAS / KITAP / KIMS**
  - KITAS: Kartu Izin Tinggal Terbatas
  - KITAP: Kartu Izin Tinggal Tetap
  - KIMS: Kartu Izin Tinggal Sementara

**Format:**
- High-quality scan
- Valid residence permit
- Must be currently valid

---

### C. Business Entity (PT, CV, PMA)

**Dokumen yang Dibutuhkan:**
1. ✅ **Latest Company Deed (Akta Pendirian Perusahaan Terbaru)**
   - Akta pendirian terbaru
   - Atau akta perubahan terakhir

2. ✅ **Decree of Ministry of Justice and Human Rights**
   - SK Kemenkumham tentang Akta Pendirian/Perubahan
   - Pengesahan dari Kementerian Hukum dan HAM

3. ✅ **Director's ID**
   - KTP Direktur (untuk WNI)
   - Passport (untuk WNA)

4. ✅ **Director's Tax ID (NPWP)**
   - NPWP Direktur pribadi

5. ✅ **Company Tax ID (NPWP)**
   - NPWP Perusahaan

6. ✅ **NIB / SIUP / TDP**
   - NIB (Nomor Induk Berusaha) - Recommended
   - SIUP (Surat Izin Usaha Perdagangan) - Legacy
   - TDP (Tanda Daftar Perusahaan) - Legacy

7. ✅ **Other Business Licenses**
   - Sesuai dengan aktivitas bisnis
   - Contoh: 
     - Izin BPOM untuk makanan/kosmetik
     - Izin edar untuk obat-obatan
     - Izin operasional khusus

**Additional Requirements for Foreign Companies:**
- ✅ Owner's/Director's Passport
- ✅ Owner's/Director's Tax ID (NPWP)
- ✅ Front page of bank book OR company bank mutation screenshot
- ✅ Company Registration Documents (dari negara asal)
- ✅ Company Deed Number
- ✅ Deed of Incorporation
- ✅ Investor Information Documents
- ✅ Business License Document (issued by country of origin)

## 🌐 3. WEBSITE / APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS

### A. Mandatory Website Criteria

#### 1. ✅ Live & Publicly Accessible
- Website harus **LIVE** dan **bisa diakses publik**
- Bukan halaman "coming soon" atau under construction
- Bukan placeholder atau demo site
- Memiliki domain yang proper (tidak .github.io atau free hosting)

#### 2. ✅ Fully Functioning Checkout Flow
- Harus ada **alur checkout yang berfungsi**
- Customer dapat:
  - Browse products/services
  - Add to cart
  - Proceed to checkout
  - See payment options
  - Complete payment (even in test mode)

#### 3. ✅ Clear Product/Service Descriptions
- **Deskripsi produk/layanan yang jelas**
- Pricing information yang transparan
- Product images (jika applicable)
- Service details dan benefits
- Package/plan comparisons

#### 4. ✅ Visible Contact Details
Harus ada informasi kontak yang **jelas dan mudah ditemukan**:
- ✅ **Email address** (professional email, bukan gmail biasa kalau bisnis besar)
- ✅ **Phone number** (dengan kode negara +62)
- ✅ **Physical address** atau kota operasional
- ✅ **Business hours** (optional but recommended)
- ✅ **Social media links** (optional but adds credibility)

**Lokasi yang Disarankan:**
- Footer di setiap halaman
- Dedicated "Contact Us" page
- Header/navigation menu

#### 5. ✅ Legal Pages (MANDATORY)

**A. Terms & Conditions (Syarat & Ketentuan)**
Harus mencakup:
- User agreement
- Service terms
- User responsibilities
- Payment terms
- Limitation of liability
- Dispute resolution
- Governing law (Indonesian law)

**B. Privacy Policy (Kebijakan Privasi)**
Harus mencakup:
- Data collection practices
- How data is used
- Data storage & security
- Third-party sharing
- User rights (GDPR-compliant is bonus)
- Contact for privacy concerns
- Compliance with Indonesian data protection laws

**C. Refund/Cancellation Policy (Kebijakan Pengembalian Dana)**
Harus mencakup:
- Refund conditions
- Cancellation process
- Timeframe for refunds
- Exceptions to refund policy
- How to request refund
- Processing time

**D. Contact Us Page**
Harus ada halaman dedicated untuk:
- Contact form (functional)
- Email address
- Phone number
- Physical address/location
- Business hours
- Map (optional but good)

#### 6. ✅ SSL/TLS Security (HTTPS)
- **MANDATORY**: Semua halaman harus HTTPS
- SSL certificate yang valid
- Terutama untuk halaman payment
- Menunjukkan "Secure" di browser

#### 7. ✅ Accurate Merchant Name
- Nama merchant di website harus **match** dengan dokumen legal
- Konsisten di semua tempat:
  - Website header/footer
  - About page
  - Legal documents
  - Payment page
  - Registration documents

#### 8. ✅ Payment Information Display
Harus jelas menampilkan:
- **Pricing** yang transparan
- **Shipping costs** (jika ada)
- **Taxes/VAT** (jika applicable)
- **Additional fees** (jika ada)
- **Total amount** sebelum payment
- **Currency** (IDR)

#### 9. ✅ No Prohibited Content
- Tidak ada konten yang melanggar aturan Midtrans
- Tidak ada prohibited products/services
- Konten appropriate dan legal
- Sesuai dengan ketentuan Indonesia

### B. For Mobile Applications

**Additional Requirements:**
- ✅ **Public app listing**
  - Google Play Store link
  - Apple App Store link
  - Atau test account yang bisa diakses tim Midtrans

- ✅ **Clear app/store links**
  - Link ke download app
  - QR code untuk download (optional)

- ✅ **Working in-app payment flow**
  - Demo atau test mode available
  - Payment integration visible
  - Checkout process clear

- ✅ **Proper app metadata**
  - App description lengkap
  - Screenshots yang jelas
  - Privacy policy linked
  - Contact developer info

## 🔐 4. COMPLIANCE & SECURITY REQUIREMENTS

### A. KYC (Know Your Customer) Requirements

**Midtrans (dan payment facilitators pada umumnya) harus comply dengan:**

1. ✅ **Customer Identification Procedures**
   - Verify identity pelanggan
   - Collect required information
   - Validate documents

2. ✅ **Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance**
   - Monitor suspicious transactions
   - Report to authorities jika perlu
   - Prevent money laundering activities

3. ✅ **OFAC Screening & Verification**
   - Screen against sanctioned lists
   - Verify customer legitimacy
   - Compliance with international regulations

### B. Technical Security Standards

**Midtrans sudah comply dengan:**

1. ✅ **PCI-DSS Level 1 Compliance**
   - Highest level of payment card security
   - Annual assessment oleh certified auditors
   - Secure card data handling

2. ✅ **ISO 27001 Certification**
   - Information security management
   - International standard compliance
   - Regular security audits

3. ✅ **Data Security**
   - Encrypted data transmission
   - Secure storage
   - Regular security updates
   - Fraud detection systems

**Yang Merchant Perlu Lakukan:**
- ✅ Implement HTTPS/SSL
- ✅ Secure customer data
- ✅ Don't store sensitive payment info
- ✅ Follow PCI-DSS guidelines untuk merchant
- ✅ Regular security updates

## 📋 6. REGISTRATION PROCESS

### Step 1: Prepare Documents
- [ ] Gather all required legal documents
- [ ] Scan high-quality copies
- [ ] Prepare digital files (PDF preferred)
- [ ] Verify all documents are valid

### Step 2: Prepare Website
- [ ] Ensure website is fully functional
- [ ] Complete all legal pages
- [ ] Install SSL certificate
- [ ] Test checkout flow
- [ ] Verify contact information

### Step 3: Register Account
1. Go to: https://dashboard.midtrans.com/register
2. Click "Register Via Email" atau "Register Via Google"
3. Fill registration form:
   - Business name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Website URL
   - Business type
4. Verify email address

### Step 4: Complete Business Profile
1. Login to Midtrans Dashboard
2. Look for banner: "One more step! Complete your registration process here"
3. Upload required documents:
   - Legal documents (KTP, NPWP, Company docs)
   - Business information
   - Bank account details
4. Submit for review

### Step 5: Wait for Approval
- Midtrans team will review application
- May take 1-3 business days
- They may contact you untuk additional information
- Be responsive to their inquiries

### Step 6: Integration & Testing
1. Receive Sandbox credentials
2. Integrate Midtrans Snap
3. Test payment flow thoroughly
4. Get Production credentials
5. Deploy to production

4. **❌ No Clear Product/Service**
   - Generic descriptions
   - No pricing information
   - Unclear offerings
   - **Solution**: Detail all products/services

5. **❌ Prohibited Business Type**
   - Gambling, adult content, etc.
   - Illegal products
   - Dangerous goods
   - **Solution**: Ensure business is allowed

6. **❌ Incomplete Legal Documents**
   - Missing required documents
   - Expired documents
   - Low quality scans
   - **Solution**: Prepare all documents properly

7. **❌ No Functioning Payment Flow**
   - Broken checkout
   - No payment demonstration
   - Unclear payment process
   - **Solution**: Implement & test checkout thoroughly

8. **❌ Missing/Unclear Contact Info**
   - No email or phone
   - Invalid contact details
   - Hard to find contact page
